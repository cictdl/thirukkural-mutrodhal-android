About this policy

This policy explains how the Android app திருக்குறள் முற்றோதல் (Recitation of Entire Tirukkural) handles information. It applies from version 2.0.0 and is effective from 28 October 2026.

"We", "us" and "CICT" mean the Central Institute of Classical Tamil. "The app" means this app.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information through the app, and we do not share or sell any data.

  • There is no account or sign-in. The app never asks for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and shows no ads.
  • The app has no crash-reporting service of its own. If you have allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may send us anonymous crash and performance statistics through the Play Console. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy.
  • CICT runs no server for this app, so nothing you do in the app reaches us.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a little data on your phone so it can play offline and remember your place. None of it is sent to CICT.

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the small settings and index files in your device backup to your Google account. Downloaded audio is always left out of backups. Google encrypts and manages these backups, and CICT cannot access them.

Internet connections and third parties

The app goes online only to fetch recitation audio, and it connects to the Internet Archive, not to CICT.

  • Internet Archive (archive.org) hosts the 133 recordings. When you play or download a chapter, your phone requests that MP3 file from archive.org over HTTPS. Like any website, the Internet Archive's servers receive your IP address, the file requested and the app's name and version. The Internet Archive handles this under its Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Copyright Policy.
  • Google Play installs and updates the app. Google's privacy policy covers what Google Play collects.
  • Android media controls show the chapter title and artwork in the notification, on the lock screen and on connected Bluetooth devices such as car stereos. This stays between your phone and those devices.

Chapters already saved on your phone play without any internet connection.

App permissions

The app uses four kinds of permission, all for playing audio. It does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone, SMS, or access to your photos and files.

PermissionWhat it is used for

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and is meant for students as well as adults. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It has no ads, in-app purchases, chat, or sign-in, and no links to social media.

Your choices

All the app's data is on your phone, so you can remove it yourself at any time. There is no account to delete and no data held by CICT to request.

  1. Remove downloaded audio: open the ⋮ menu on the home screen and choose பதிவிறக்கங்களை நீக்கு (Remove downloads). To remove one chapter, tap the ✓ beside it and choose பதிவிறக்கத்தை நீக்கு.
  2. Clear everything the app stores: go to Android Settings → Apps → திருக்குறள் முற்றோதல் → Storage → Clear storage.
  3. Uninstall the app: this removes all of its data from your phone.

Security

All audio is fetched over encrypted HTTPS connections. Everything the app stores sits in its private app storage, which other apps on your phone cannot read.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, we will update this policy and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
